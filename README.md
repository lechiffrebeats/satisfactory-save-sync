## 🏭 Modded Satisfactory Auto-Sync

turn GitHub into a free, automatic **Async Server** for your friend group.

---

### ⚠️ THE GOLDEN RULE

**Only ONE person plays at a time!** The script locks the world to prevent "timeline splits." If it's locked, wait for your friend to finish or join his session.

---

### 🛠️ Quick Setup (5 Minutes)

1. **Install:** [GitHub Desktop](https://desktop.github.com/) and [Git for Windows](https://gitforwindows.org/).
2. **Clone:** Clone this repo to your PC.
3. **Config:** Duplicate `config.template.ps1` → rename to **`config.ps1`**. Add your SteamID and PC Name.
4. **Files:** Drop your `.sav` and `.smmprofile` into the **`system/save/`** folder. [cite: 2026-02-16]

---

### 🎮 How to Play

**Do not** open Steam or the Mod Manager directly. Use these:

* 🚀 **`Play_Modded.bat`**: Syncs everything and opens the Mod Manager.
* 🚀 **`Play_Vanilla.bat`**: Syncs everything and opens Steam.

**The script handles everything:**

* **Downloads** the latest world before you play.
* **Uploads** your progress to GitHub when you quit.
* **Alerts** you if a friend updated the modpack.
